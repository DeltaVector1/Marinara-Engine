# Product

## Purpose

Marinara Engine is a personal chat and roleplay workspace. It runs on the user's device and connects to local or hosted language models. It helps one person configure models, manage characters and chats, tune prompts, and read or shape long conversations.

## Primary user

A person who wants direct control over their chat environment, data, characters, and model settings. They may use simple defaults or advanced prompt, lorebook, agent, and provider tools.

## Core workflows

- Connect a local or hosted model.
- Create or import a character and start a Conversation or Roleplay chat.
- Read, continue, edit, and organize a conversation.
- Add optional context through personas, lorebooks, presets, and supported agents.

## Product principles

1. Keep the conversation as the main workspace. Reading and writing should take priority over decoration and setup.
2. Make controls clear and labelled. Put frequent actions near the chat and keep rare settings in focused panels.
3. Show advanced controls when they become useful. Use sensible defaults without hiding the user's ability to inspect and change them.
4. Keep chat and saved data on the user's device. Explain when a request goes to an external model provider.
5. Keep the same core workflows usable with a keyboard, assistive technology, and a small touch screen.

## Voice

Use direct, practical language. Prefer short labels, clear status, and concise explanations. Avoid marketing copy, game framing, and decorative language that competes with the conversation.
