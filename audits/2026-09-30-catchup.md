## Local review

The first local CodeRabbit attempt did not complete: the review service closed its WebSocket during connection. The retry connected but rejected the 203-file scope because the current free open-source limit is 150 files. Neither rejected attempt counts as completed review. The changes were then split into bounded committed batches without using paid credits.

The first batch (`2ba5d1dad..b80307e75`, 143 files: seven packs and audit records) completed with three minor findings:

- Korean feature-guide defaults: the opening paragraph follows the English source's first-use/default description. The same guide's Precedence section explicitly describes environment overrides and saved values. `isFeatureEnabled()` in `packages/server/src/services/features/feature-settings.ts` confirms environment, saved value, then default. The proposed broader qualification is an English editorial follow-up, not a Korean-only correction; the documented precedence remains intact in every pack.
- French Decision-server setup: Android defaults the local-URL flag on when unset. `isProviderLocalUrlsEnabled()` in `packages/server/src/config/runtime-config.ts` confirms this, and the linked French local/self-hosted guide already states that exception. The translated Decision paragraph matches the English paragraph and its cross-reference. Making the exception inline belongs in the English source and a coordinated follow-up, rather than changing only French.
- Korean foundations overview: the English introductory sentence says D-H each add switch rows, while its D/E sections explicitly describe switch-free bug fixes. The translation retains both statements and the historical series-plan context. This is an English-source consistency follow-up, not a translation regression.

These are documented dispositions, not zero findings. Integration review also clarified Russian minimum-API wording and corrected a Hindi heading. The second batch includes the three remaining packs, that heading/manifest correction, and this review record (63 files); review is pending.
